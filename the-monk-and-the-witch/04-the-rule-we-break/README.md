# The Rule We Break

[<img src="cover.webp" alt="Cover of The Rule We Break" width="420">](cover.webp)

After bloodshed in a sacred grove, the Twilight-Stream family takes in Rishi and Maeril, leaving them to earn the trust of people with every reason to fear them. When a child vanishes during the Feast of the Moon, they must follow a fracture in the forest’s ancient magic into the Wealdath.
