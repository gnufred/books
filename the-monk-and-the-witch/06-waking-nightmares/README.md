# Waking Nightmares

[<img src="cover.webp" alt="Cover of Waking Nightmares" width="420">](cover.webp)

Firemind’s return sends Rishi and Maeril back to Waterdeep with Elisa, where Manshoon and Xanathar are hunting the Stone of Golorr and the vault it can reveal. As Xanathar’s nightmares spill into the waking world, they must find the Stone before either rival reaches the gold and the city collapses over the struggle below.
